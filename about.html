About Papakid

A gents salon built around neat detail and reliable care.

Papakid Gents Salon provides everyday grooming and treatment services for clients who want a sharp haircut, well-shaped beard, refreshed skin, or a full-care appointment in Al Warqa 1.

Our Approach

Professional service with a clear, straightforward menu.

The salon menu is organized around the services clients request most: haircuts and treatments, beard shaping, skin care, facials, hand and foot care, and spa add-ons. Each service is presented with transparent pricing so visitors can plan before they arrive.

The brand style is confident and direct: black textured finishes, strong gold accents, and clean white typography, matching the original Papakid service card.
